# FreshRoute — Cutting Food Waste from Farm to Shelf

## The problem

Every year, grocery retailers throw away nearly a third of the fresh produce
they buy. The cause is rarely bad food — it's bad timing. Delivery trucks
arrive out of order, stock rotates incorrectly, and perfectly good vegetables
expire in the back room while older stock sits untouched on the shelf. For a
mid-sized grocery chain this waste quietly burns two to four percent of
revenue, and it's one of the largest avoidable sources of retail carbon
emissions.

## The solution

FreshRoute is a logistics brain for perishable goods. Small battery-powered
sensors ride along inside produce crates, tracking temperature and humidity
from the farm gate to the store shelf. Our routing engine combines that live
freshness data with traffic and weather to answer one question continuously:
which crate should go where, right now? Trucks get rerouted mid-journey,
stores receive shelf-rotation instructions each morning, and produce that
won't sell in time is automatically offered to discount partners and food
banks before it spoils.

## Traction

In a six-month pilot with two regional chains covering forty-one stores,
FreshRoute cut produce waste by 38 percent and increased fresh-goods margin
by 2.1 points. Both pilots converted to annual contracts. The sensor hardware
pays for itself in eleven weeks.

year, and regulators in the EU are beginning to fine large retailers for
